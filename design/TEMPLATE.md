# Decision Title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Proposed
**Owner:** Accountable individual or team
**Review trigger:** Condition that requires this record to be reviewed

## Problem

Describe the decision that is needed and the problem it must solve.

## Context

Describe the relevant system, constraints, assumptions, and prior decisions.

## Goals

- State the measurable or observable outcomes this decision must achieve.

## Non-Goals

- State what this decision intentionally does not address.

## Decision

Describe the chosen approach and why it satisfies the goals.

## Alternatives and Trade-Offs

For each credible alternative, describe its benefits, costs, risks, and why it
was not selected.

## Consequences

Describe the expected positive and negative effects, including follow-up work,
compatibility implications, and operational impact.

## Implementation Outline

List the bounded implementation steps, affected interfaces, and ownership.

## Rollout

Describe the safe introduction sequence, prerequisites, monitoring, and any
compatibility period.

## Validation

Define the automated checks, manual checks, and acceptance criteria that prove
the decision was implemented correctly.

## Rollback

Define the conditions that require rollback and the verified recovery strategy.
If a rollback strategy is not yet verified, state that explicitly before
implementation begins.

## Security, Performance, and Accessibility Considerations

Assess security boundaries, sensitive data, performance or scalability impact,
and accessibility impact. Record why any consideration is not applicable.

## References

Link related issues, pull requests, documents, source files, and superseding or
superseded records.
